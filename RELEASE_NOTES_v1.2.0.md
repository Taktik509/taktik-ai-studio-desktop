# Taktik AI Studio v1.2.0 — Desktop app

Native desktop apps for **Windows**, **macOS** (Intel + Apple Silicon) and **Linux**.
No browser needed — open your Taktik workspace directly on your computer.

## 🆕 NEW in v1.2.0

- **Navigation toolbar** — Back, Forward, Reload and Home buttons always visible at the top (plus `Alt+Left` / `Alt+Right` shortcuts)
- **Google & Facebook login fixed** — social sign-in now opens in a secure in-app popup and returns you to the app logged in
- **Windows installer** — choose between the portable version (unzip & run) and the classic installer (`.exe` with Start menu shortcuts)

## 🛠️ Offline Toolkit (works without internet)

- **Hook Lab** — 10 proven short-form video hook formulas; fill in the blanks, copy your hook
- **CTA Builder** — 5 call-to-action archetypes with templates
- **Playbooks** — 5 niche marketing playbooks (pain formulas + ready-to-run prompt templates)
- **Content Calendar** — plan your month, ideas saved on your computer
- **Caption Formatter** — live counters, line-break fixer, hashtag extractor

Find it in the **Tools** menu — online or off.

## Online (needs internet)

All AI-powered tools (Hook Bank AI, UGC Factory, Listing SEO, TikTok Shop Creator,
Digital Product Creator, Flow Studio, …), your credits, billing and account —
same as the website, same login.
